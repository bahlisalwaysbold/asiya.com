Professional profile

From benefits administration to business development.

Asiya is a Sociology graduate whose pension career began with the National Social Insurance Trust Fund during NYSC. She then moved into benefits administration at Trustfund Pensions, where her work included computation, before progressing into RSA transfer operations and business development.

At CrusaderSterling Pensions she worked as a Business Development Executive. She is currently with Citizens Pensions as a Team Lead / Business Development Executive.
